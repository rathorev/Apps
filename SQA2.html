Smart Quiz Studio – User Guide & Specifications

1. Dual Operating Modes

Practice Mode: Immediate feedback on every answer submission, verified answer keys, detailed explanations, hints, and active question re-queuing.

Exam Mode: Formal test simulation. Answers and feedback are hidden during testing. A comprehensive breakdown with scores and explanations is presented upon exam completion.

2. Enforce Learning Algorithm

When enabled, questions answered incorrectly are automatically pushed to the back of the pending queue. They will reappear only after other questions have been answered, ensuring spaced repetition and proven retention before a question is considered Mastered.

3. CSV Import & Format Specifications

You can import any custom question bank using standard CSV files. Supported metadata headers:

# Category: [TOPIC CATEGORY]
# Heading: [MAIN HEADING]
# Subheading: [SUB-SCOPE]
ID,Type,Question,Options,CorrectAnswer,Explanation,Hint

Type encodings: MC (Multiple Choice), MS (Multiple Select with pipe delimiters), TF (True/False), and FB (Fill in the Blank).

4. LocalStorage & Auto-Persistence

All progress, active session indexes, answer histories, and question banks are automatically saved to LocalStorage and the notebook shim. You can safely close or refresh your browser and resume seamlessly.
